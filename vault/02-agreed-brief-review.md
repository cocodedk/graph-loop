## Goal

Compose the agreed brief from the recorded decisions, have an independent reviewer check it, let the user confirm it, and deliver it to branch writing and specification.

## Why

Given a set of recorded decisions, a brief appears with the outcome, scope, accepted decisions (each with a stable identifier), examples, constraints, exclusions and explicitly delegated choices. An independent reviewer compares it with the original request, the recorded answers and the evidence. Each blocking finding names the missing decision and its consequence. The brief is not ready while a consequential question is open, a contradiction remains, acceptance cannot be observed, or the review finds a blocking ambiguity. A time limit, question limit, empty queue or high Jev confidence never makes it ready. The user confirms a specific revision, and confirming does not start execution or approve a campaign. The confirmed brief and its revision are handed explicitly to the branch writer, the spec writer and their reviewers, and not carried only as a goal string. Specifications name the decisions they implement. Changing a confirmed answer requires a new readiness check and review of the material that depends on it.
